# Landing media

The images were created with the built-in image generation tool for the CareerOS landing page. They are illustrative, not photographs of actual users or their work. Optimized locally to WebP with Sharp; originals remain in the image generation output directory.

- `alpine-hero.webp`: 2400 × 1351; fallback when WebGL is unavailable or loading. The live mountain is procedural Three.js geometry in `components/landing/mountain-scene.tsx`.
- `work-evidence.webp`: 1400 × 1050; project-work editorial image.

## Alpine image prompt

Create a high resolution panoramic 16:9 website hero background photograph, no typography, no logos, no interface. A monumental finely detailed snowy alpine peak placed in the right third of the frame at x=72%, rising from a vast ocean of pale mist. A subtle second ridge in far background on the left at low height. Foreground fog covers the entire bottom quarter, dissolving into almost white #eef3f5. The left 50% of the image is very calm pale blue-gray sky and mist with lots of clean negative space for dark navy headlines overlayed later. Premium art-directed realistic aerial landscape photography, sophisticated minimal composition, restrained cool silver and glacier blue tones, very soft dawn light, extraordinarily crisp rock and snow detail on the main right peak, strong natural sense of spatial depth. Mountain summit around 33% from top; white cloud wisps below. No forest, no lake, no buildings, no people, no flags, no sun disk, no bright blue saturated sky, no purple, no text. More photographic and restrained than a fantasy landscape. The image is for CareerOS, a career-planning platform whose metaphor is a clear path to your next summit.

## Project-work image prompt

Generate a premium editorial photograph for a minimal career-planning website, landscape 4:3 composition. Close-up view across a quiet contemporary designer's work desk beside a large window, soft natural overcast daylight. A person's hands in a dark navy sweater sketching a simple website wireframe in an open cream notebook with a pencil; a slim silver laptop seen from its side with an out-of-focus code editor and simple interface preview, no readable text. A few neatly stacked design sketches and a brushed steel water glass. Pale stone desktop, cool blue-gray and warm neutral color palette, tactile paper texture, authentic work in progress, calm refined art direction, beautifully composed natural photograph with subtle film detail and shallow depth of field. No faces, no logos, no decorative plants, no glowing purple lights, no generic stock-photo handshakes, no artificial icons or overlays. Main hands and notebook in lower middle, laptop on left, airy light from upper right. The image communicates evidence of real project work and learning. No words or text.
